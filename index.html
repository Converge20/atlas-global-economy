05 / About the data

Built on real economic indicators

Data comes from the World Bank Open Data API. Figures are reported as published, with missing values left blank. GDP in current US dollars is nominal, not inflation-adjusted. The country leaderboard excludes World Bank regional and income-group aggregates. World totals are not calculated by adding incomplete country observations.

Use the indicator selector, interactive map, timeline, and comparison charts to explore how economies differ. Data availability and revisions vary by indicator and country; this prototype currently covers 2000–2024 in its main dashboard.

Official exchange-rate explorer

World Bank indicator PA.NUS.FCRF represents the official exchange rate, in local currency units per US dollar, as an annual period average. This is not a real-time FX quote.
