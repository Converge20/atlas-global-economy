05 / About the data

Built on real economic indicators

Data comes from the World Bank Open Data API. Figures are reported as published, with missing values left blank. GDP in current US dollars is nominal, not inflation-adjusted. The country leaderboard excludes World Bank regional and income-group aggregates. World totals are not calculated by adding incomplete country observations.

Use the indicator selector, interactive map, timeline, and comparison charts to explore how economies differ. The latest complete data year varies by indicator and country.
